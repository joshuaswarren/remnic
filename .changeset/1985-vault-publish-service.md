Add the markdown-vault publisher for timeline artifacts (issue #1985): `remnic timeline publish [--date YYYY-MM-DD] [--dry-run] [--what timeline]` writes the day's persisted timeline recap into the user's markdown vault daily note through strictly-bounded managed regions. Everything outside the region — frontmatter, human sections, other agents' sections, CRLF/LF — is byte-identical after publish; unchanged content produces no write; all writes are temp-file + rename in the note's directory; `--dry-run` performs zero writes. Config lives under `activity.timeline.vault.*` (default off; invalid values are rejected at config load naming the missing prerequisite). Path templates accept `{yyyy} {yy} {M} {MM} {d} {dd} {ww} {MMM} {MMMM} {ddd} {dddd}` with unknown tokens rejected naming the full valid set. `properties.mode: "frontmatter"` edits only prefix-owned frontmatter keys via targeted line edits; `"dataview-inline"` appends inline fields inside the region. Crossed begin/end marker pairs and symlinked paths resolving outside the vault are refused without touching the file. The standup, weekly, and locations publish targets are rejected with a named reason until their phases land; the publisher mechanism is live for the timeline target.

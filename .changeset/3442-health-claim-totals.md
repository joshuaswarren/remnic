Stability: stable

Report the claimed drain backlog in the `/engram/v1/health` extraction block (issue #3442). `extraction.pendingTurnCount` counts only the in-memory buffer, so turns handed to `<stateDir>/buffer/drain` claims were invisible: on jarvis-ml health reported 10,512 pending turns while 2,328 claims held 317,246 pending turns (30x more) with `degraded: false`. Now: claim `*.json.count` sidecars gain an `oldestTurnAt` field (back-compatible — older sidecars simply lack it), `BufferDrainClaims.claimTotals()` serves a TTL-cached (30s), bounded (4,096 bodies) sidecar-only scan that never parses a claim body, and `/health.extraction` adds `claimedSessionCount`, `claimedPendingTurnCount`, `oldestClaimedTurnAgeMs`, `oldestClaimedAgeBasis`, and `claimedBacklogTruncated`. Liveness also degrades — with a distinct `claimed drain backlog: …` reason that appends the last extraction-LLM failure — when claimed turns exist and the oldest has waited longer than `extractionLiveness.staleWindowMs` (half-open window, §23). In-memory `pendingTurnCount` semantics are unchanged.

Because every sidecar on existing hosts predates `oldestTurnAt`, the oldest-age rule would be inert on exactly the backlog it targets. For a claim whose sidecar lacks a usable `oldestTurnAt`, the scan therefore falls back to the claim body's mtime as the oldest-age candidate: every claim-body mutation is a whole-file rewrite, so `mtime >= newest turn >= oldest turn` and `now - mtime` is a TRUE LOWER BOUND on the oldest pending turn's age — no extra I/O (the body is already stated for the sidecar identity check). The minimum is taken across claims with pending > 0 (drained tombstones are ignored), and `oldestClaimedAgeBasis` (`"turn"` or `"claim-mtime-lower-bound"`) says how the reported age was derived: with the lower-bound basis the true backlog age is at least the reported one, so staleness decisions made on it are conservative in the safe direction. On jarvis-ml, ~313k of the 317k claimed turns sit in claims whose last write is more than 1 day old, so health will correctly flag the backlog from day one.

Two incompleteness counters keep the pending sum honest: `claimedBacklogPendingInbox` counts claims with a non-empty hand-in inbox (turns appended after the last sidecar write are pending but absent from the sidecar counts; each such claim also contributes its inbox mtime as a lower-bound age candidate — appends only ever add content, so every inbox turn predates the last append), and `claimedBacklogUnverified` counts claims whose sidecar was missing or stale. Both are summed out of `claimedPendingTurnCount` by design; they tell an operator when (and which way) the pending sum undercounts.

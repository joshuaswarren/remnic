## Evidence discipline

All Relay live inputs and outputs use freshly provisioned synthetic roots. No
production Remnic memory, shared context, personal data, or ordinary Codex home
is read or written by the mission. A visible receipt may claim only what a
fixture, captured structured output, source file, memory record, recall event,
approval, or executed test proves. Raw prompts, transcripts, Codex JSONL,
credentials, private ledgers, and benchmark datasets are excluded.

The user's remaining authority is preserved: final video recording/upload,
selection of the real `/feedback` session, and Devpost submission.
